HIGH CARD FLUSH — RULES AND STRATEGY
====================================

High Card Flush is a seven-card table game played against the dealer. The
longest group of cards in one suit wins. If both hands have the same number of
suited cards, compare those cards from highest to lowest until one hand wins.
All suits are equal. Ace is high.

This browser version uses virtual chips for entertainment and practice only.

1. WAGERS AND DEAL
==================

- Place the required Ante before the deal.
- Flush Bonus and Straight Flush Bonus are optional and use only your seven cards.
- The table keeps enough bankroll available for at least a 1x Raise.
- You and the dealer each receive seven cards from a freshly shuffled 52-card deck.
- Your cards are shown immediately. The dealer's cards stay face down until you Raise.

2. CHOOSE THE BEST FLUSH
========================

Your best flush is the largest number of cards you hold in one suit. A four-card
flush always beats a three-card flush, regardless of the individual ranks.

When two flushes have the same length, compare the highest card in each flush.
If those tie, compare the next card, continuing until the tie is broken. Exact
matches push because suits do not break ties.

3. FOLD OR RAISE
================

After seeing your seven cards, choose Fold or place a Raise wager.

FLUSH LENGTH       ALLOWED RAISE
--------------------------------
2, 3, or 4 cards   1x Ante
5 cards            1x or 2x Ante
6 or 7 cards       1x, 2x, or 3x Ante

Folding forfeits the Ante. Optional bonus wagers are still evaluated and can
still win after a fold.

4. DEALER QUALIFICATION AND BASE PAYOUTS
========================================

The dealer qualifies with a three-card, 9-high flush or any stronger flush.

DEALER DOES NOT QUALIFY
- Ante wins and pays 1 to 1.
- Raise pushes and is returned.

DEALER QUALIFIES
- Higher player flush: Ante and Raise each pay 1 to 1.
- Exact tie: Ante and Raise both push.
- Lower player flush: Ante and Raise both lose.

5. FLUSH BONUS — PAYTABLE A
===========================

YOUR LONGEST FLUSH    PAYS
--------------------------
7 cards               300 to 1
6 cards               100 to 1
5 cards                10 to 1
4 cards                 1 to 1
3 cards or fewer        Loses

6. STRAIGHT FLUSH BONUS — PAYTABLE A
====================================

The longest consecutive run in any one suit determines this bonus. Ace is high
only, so Q-K-A counts as consecutive but A-2-3 does not.

LONGEST STRAIGHT FLUSH    PAYS
------------------------------
7 cards                    8,000 to 1
6 cards                    1,000 to 1
5 cards                      100 to 1
4 cards                       60 to 1
3 cards                        7 to 1
2 cards or fewer              Loses

The straight flush may be in a different suit from the flush used against the
dealer. Bonus results never depend on the dealer's hand.

7. SIMPLIFIED STRATEGY HINT
===========================

The optional hint uses a close-to-optimal published strategy:

- Raise the maximum allowed amount with every four-card or longer flush.
- With a three-card flush, Raise 1x on T-8-6 or better.
- Fold lower three-card flushes and all two-card flushes.

For the T-8-6 comparison, evaluate all three cards from highest to lowest. For
example, J-3-2 is above the threshold because the jack beats the ten; T-8-5 is
below it because the first two cards tie and five loses to six.
