Design mockups · fantasy league hub

Team view, three directions

The league page became a ledger — serif masthead, one table, hairline rules. The team view is still the older card stack: a head card, an overview card, a start/sit card, then results and roster side by side. These three studies each pick a different thing for the team page to be about.

The team ledger

01

Carries the league page’s language down one level. The team gets its own masthead and standing line, and the roster becomes a single table with a switchable column — projection, position rank, last week, season average, market — the way the league ledger switches its own. Results become a margin chart instead of a list.

Most continuous with the hub Roster is the centrepiece Least changed in shape

Sunday desk

02

Decision-first. A verdict bar states the week’s gain in one line, the lineup renders slot by slot the way a lineup actually looks, and each suggested change rides directly under the slot it would replace. The season, the overview, and the power trend move to a rail. Bench is sorted by what each player would add, not by position.

Best on a Sunday morning One clear action per row Season facts demoted

Season dossier

03

The written overview leads, set as a real lede with the record beside it as a colophon. The season is a week-by-week timeline with margin bars and opponent records, and the roster is grouped by room — each position measured against the league average for that position. Start/sit survives as a small aside.

Best for reading a rival Gives the overview a job Weakest for acting
