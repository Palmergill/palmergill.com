    How this number is built

    Uses 1 point per 25 passing yards, 4 per passing touchdown, 1 per 10 rushing or receiving yards, and 6 per rushing or receiving touchdown. A category scores only when both its yardage and touchdown markets are quoted, so a running quarterback with no rushing-touchdown line is scored on passing alone — each row names what went into its total, and flags a category that had to be dropped. Half PPR and PPR add 0.5 or 1 point per projected reception. Interceptions, two-point conversions, and fumbles are not included, which flatters quarterbacks most.

    Market-implied values, contract lines, and displayed prices are for information only — not betting advice. Availability varies by player and source, and each source is shown with when it last moved a price: a season-long market can sit untouched for weeks.
