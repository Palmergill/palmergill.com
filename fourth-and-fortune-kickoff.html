The game

It's push-your-luck with a 52-card deck

Flip cards to build a pot. Bank it and it's yours forever. Flip one too many and it's gone.

FLIP

Add to your pot

Turn over the next card from your deck. Its value goes into this round's pot.

BUST

Match a rank, lose it all

Flip a rank you already hold this round and the round is over. Zero. Suit doesn't save you — a pair of 3s is a pair of 3s.

BANK

Lock it in

Stop, bank the pot, add it to your total. Banked points can never be taken away.

That's the whole game. Every hard decision you make tonight is just "one more?"

Structure

Five rounds, and the leader goes first

Everyone plays a round before anyone starts the next one. No hiding at the back.

  • Round 1: turn order comes from the seed. Pure luck, no complaints.
  • Rounds 2–5: order is frozen from the standings after the previous round — whoever's leading goes first.
  • That's the punishment for being ahead: you act with no information, and nine people watch you decide.
  • Rounds 1 through 4 are open table and pay face value. Every card, every pot, every bank — live, in front of the league.
  • Round 5 is sealed and pays 2×. Rounds 1–4 are you setting up. Round 5 is the one that hurts.

Yes, that means the leader banks first and everyone behind them gets a number to chase. That's the point. And going into round 5, that lead is worth half what they think it is.

Round five

The blackout round — and it pays double

The last round is played in the dark, and it is worth twice what it says.

×2

Double or nothing

Whatever you bank in round 5 counts twice. A strong round lands near 40, so under flat scoring anyone ~25 back was already mathematically out while still being made to play. Now nobody is out.

HIDDEN

You see nothing

During round 5 you only see how many cards your opponents have flipped. Not their cards. Not their pot. Not their total.

FROZEN

The board stops

The public leaderboard freezes at the round-4 standings. No "score to beat," no bank position. You are flying blind.

REVEAL

All at once

When the last manager finishes, the commissioner triggers the reveal. Final scores and the full draft order drop on everyone at the same second.

Do the math before you flip: if you're 30 points down, you don't need 30 — you need to out-bank the leader by 15. That's a real shot. Play it like one.

Scoring

How ties get broken

Three steps, in order, no debate.

3Seeded tiebreak — HMAC-SHA256 off the master seedLocked before a single card was dealt
  • Busted round = 0. It still counts as one of your five. And zero doubled is still zero — busting round 5 costs you twice as much as it looks.
  • Sitting out isn't a strategy: if a manager goes dark on the clock, the commissioner can write the round off after a grace period. That round scores zero.

Fairness

You can audit every card I dealt you

This is the part where I get ahead of the group chat.

  • The master seed is committed before the room opens and published as a SHA-256 hash.
  • Your deck each round is derived from that seed, your account name, and the round number. It existed before you clicked anything.
  • The seed stays sealed until the last score is locked. Nobody — including me — can see or change a deck mid-game.
  • When it's over, the verification page hands you the seed and the recipe. Reproduce every shuffle yourself.

So when you bust on card nine holding 61 points, the math is airtight and it is entirely your fault.
