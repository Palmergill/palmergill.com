# Code Review — 2026-08-30 — Fantasy

- **Scope:** the whole fantasy surface — `fantasy/` (dashboard, `league/`,
  `rankings/`, `draft-order/`), `backend/app/routers/fantasy*.py`,
  `backend/app/services/fantasy_*.py`, and specs 16/17/18.
- **Method:** read the frontend and backend directly; ran both suites
  (jest 339/339, pytest 736/736 green); drove `/fantasy/` in a browser
  against the local backend with live collected data (`as_of` 2026-08-30);
  reproduced the backend defect against a real session.
- **Resolution note:** Findings 1, 2 and 3 were fixed on 2026-08-30 (see
  "Resolutions" below). Findings 4-10 remain open.
- **Baseline health:** good. Auth boundaries are right, the demo/member
  split holds, no XSS paths (everything goes through `textContent`), no
  mobile overflow, and the comments explain intent unusually well.

## Findings (most severe first)

### 1. Player search can't find the players people search for — CONFIRMED
- **File:** `backend/app/services/fantasy_data.py:570` (`search_players`)
- The query is a bare `LIKE '%term%'` on `search_name` ordered by
  `full_name ASC`, with no relevance ranking and no weighting toward active
  or rostered players. The dashboard requests `limit=8`.
- **Failure scenario:** typing `Allen` into the market board's search
  returns Allen Hurns, Allen Lazard, Allen Reisner, Allen Robinson,
  Allenzae Staggers, Austin Allen, Austin Allen, Braelon Allen — **not Josh
  Allen**, who is rank #1 on the board directly above the search box. He
  only appears at `limit=25`. `Smith` returns eight retired or free-agent
  players. `Hill` returns Andy Phillips (a kicker, matched because
  "phillips" contains "hill") and no Tyreek Hill. `Tucker` returns six
  no-team players before Tucker Kraft.
- **Why it bites:** the dashboard has no other way to reach a player. The
  board itself is capped at 100 rows (133 are quoted), so search is the
  only route to the tail — and it is alphabetical noise.
- **Fix direction:** rank prefix matches on last name above substring
  matches, and sort by whether the player has a team/active status before
  falling back to name. A cheap version: `ORDER BY (team IS NULL),
  (search_name NOT LIKE 'term%'), full_name`.

### 2. "Edge" compares two different scoring definitions, and the caveat
    only fires half the time — CONFIRMED
- **Files:** `backend/app/services/fantasy_data.py:1232`
  (`_score_season_player`), `fantasy/app.js:718` (`pairs.missing`),
  `fantasy/format.js` (`seasonPairDetail`)
- A market total only scores a category when **both** its yardage and
  touchdown markets are quoted. `partial_pairs` — the thing the UI renders
  as a caveat (`season-leader__gap`, "receiving not fully quoted") — is
  populated only when *one half* of a pair is quoted. When a category is
  absent **entirely**, `partial_pairs` is empty and no caveat renders at
  all. That is the common case.
- **Failure scenario (live data, 2026-08-30):** of 38 RBs on the board, 23
  are scored on rushing alone with `partial_pairs == []` — no caveat.
  Jeremiyah Love shows Market 113.0 vs Proj 199.8, **Edge −86.8**; his row
  meta reads `RB ARI · rushing · 3 sources` and nothing flags that his
  entire receiving line is missing from the total. Same for Omarion Hampton
  (−70.2), Travis Etienne (−60.8), Bo Nix (−58.0, passing-only QB, rushing
  absent). Meanwhile Jahmyr Gibbs, who *does* have both categories, shows
  Edge −8.7. Mean Edge by position: RB −33.7, WR −13.9, QB −12.9, TE −11.0
  — the RB gap is coverage, not market disagreement.
- The board's default sort is `fantasy_points` descending, so players are
  also *ranked* partly by how many categories happened to be quoted.
- **Fix direction:** report absent categories, not just half-quoted ones —
  compute the set of categories a player's position expects and name the
  ones with no quote at all, feeding both into the same
  `season-leader__gap` caveat. Consider suppressing the Edge value (or
  greying it) when the market total is missing a category the projection
  includes; an edge between incomparable totals is not an edge.

### 3. `GET /api/fantasy/games/{id}/lines/history?market=h2h` 500s —
    CONFIRMED
- **File:** `backend/app/services/fantasy_data.py:940` and `:1530`
- `_consensus_price` is **defined twice** in the same module. The second
  definition (1 arg, median in probability space) shadows the first
  (3 args, mean of matching rows) for the whole module, so the call at
  line 971 passes three arguments to a one-argument function.
- **Failure scenario:** reproduced against a real session with one
  `FantasyOddsSnapshot` row —
  `TypeError: _consensus_price() takes 1 positional argument but 3 were
  given`. The `spreads` and `totals` paths are unaffected (they use
  `_consensus_point`).
- This is the same shape as the `/trending` bug closed on 2026-08-22:
  nothing on the site calls this route and there is no test for it, so it
  has been failing silently since it was written.
- **Fix direction:** rename one of them (the line-history helper is really
  `_mean_price_for_outcome`), and add a contract test that walks all three
  `market` values. Then decide whether the route earns its keep at all —
  see finding 7.

### 4. The compare drawer mixes season-long and weekly numbers under one
    "Week 1" heading — CONFIRMED
- **File:** `fantasy/app.js:1279` (`renderCompare`)
- **Failure scenario:** comparing Josh Allen renders, top to bottom:
  `356.6 MARKET PTS` / `pass tds · pass yds · rush tds · rush yds` /
  `-8.8 vs 365.4 proj` / `23.3 WK 1 PROJ`, with the drawer subtitle reading
  `Week 1 · STD · Sleeper`. Two numbers are both labelled "proj" (365.4
  season-long, 23.3 weekly) three lines apart, and the drawer's own heading
  claims the whole panel is Week 1 while its largest number is season-long.
- **Fix direction:** label the market block's projection explicitly
  ("365.4 season proj") and change `compareSub` to name both horizons, or
  drop the weekly projection from a drawer whose headline metric is
  season-long.

### 5. The compare drawer's "not quoted" state is unreachable — CONFIRMED
- **File:** `fantasy/app.js:1314`, `backend/app/services/fantasy_data.py:780`
- `compare_players` always sets `entry["market"]` to a dict — an all-`None`
  dict for an unquoted player, never `None`. The client does
  `marketEntry ? "market pts" : "not quoted"`, and the object is always
  truthy, so the `"not quoted"` label never renders.
- **Failure scenario:** comparing Carl Tucker (no season market) against
  Josh Allen renders `— MARKET PTS` for Tucker rather than the intended
  `— NOT QUOTED`. Verified in the browser.
- `marketRowFor()` (`fantasy/app.js:1274`) is dead for the same reason —
  and its comment ("The board's payload holds every quoted player") is
  wrong regardless: the page requests `limit=100` against 133 quoted
  players.
- **Fix direction:** return `null` for `market` when nothing is quoted, or
  branch on `marketEntry.total != null` on the client.

### 6. On mobile, the category-coverage caveat is truncated on every row —
    CONFIRMED
- **File:** `fantasy/style.css:1190`
- `.season-leader__meta` gets `white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden;
  text-overflow: ellipsis` under the mobile breakpoint.
- **Failure scenario:** at 375px every one of the ten visible rows is
  clipped (`scrollWidth` 165–174px vs `clientWidth` 151px). "QB BUF ·
  passing + rushing · 3 sources" renders as "QB BUF · passing + rush…".
  The one row that fits exactly is Joe Burrow's shorter "QB CIN · passing"
  — so the *incomplete* rows are the ones that look complete. There is no
  tooltip fallback on touch. This compounds finding 2 on the platform
  where it is least recoverable.
- **Fix direction:** let the meta line wrap to two lines on mobile (row
  height is already a fixed 58px, so it needs to grow), or move category
  coverage out of the run-on meta string into its own badge.

### 7. Two endpoints have no caller on the site
- `GET /api/fantasy/games/{id}/lines/history` — nothing calls it, no test,
  and it 500s on one of its three markets (finding 3).
- `POST /api/fantasy/chat` — `fantasy/chat.js` is loaded **only** by
  `fantasy/league/index.html`, which is members-only. The public
  `/fantasy/` dashboard dropped its chat panel in the redesign
  (`8059078`), so `fantasy_ai.answer_demo_chat` — the whole demo-mode
  local router built for spec R16 — is now unreachable through the UI.
- `/api/fantasy/trending` is already logged in `BUGS.md` as the same
  question. That is three; it is worth one decision rather than three.
- **Fix direction:** either delete, or add the contract tests that would
  have caught finding 3.

### 8. Any signed-in member can force unbounded model spend
- **File:** `backend/app/routers/fantasy_league.py:215`,
  `backend/app/services/fantasy_ai.py:854`
- `POST /teams/{id}/overview?force=true` skips the `_is_fresh(row, digest)`
  cache check and calls OpenAI every time. There is no rate limit on the
  route and no rate limit on `POST /api/fantasy/chat` either.
- The digest cache exists precisely so a page view can't bill; `force`
  hands any member an unmetered override. Members are people you know, so
  this is exposure rather than a live threat — but it is the one paid path
  in the whole area with no ceiling.
- **Fix direction:** restrict `force` to admins (`is_admin`, as
  `admin/refresh` already does), or put a per-account daily cap on
  generations.

### 9. `/season-props?limit=200` is fetched on every page load for a closed
    drawer
- **File:** `fantasy/app.js:1706` (`init`)
- `loadSeasonPropLeaders()` runs unconditionally at startup, but its output
  (`#seasonPropsTabs`, `#seasonPropsLeaders`, `#seasonPropsPositions`) is
  entirely inside `#marketsDrawer`, which starts hidden. Opening the drawer
  calls it again.
- **Measured:** 34.5 KB fetched and rendered off-screen per page load,
  refetched on first "Player markets" click. Confirmed in the network log.
- **Fix direction:** call it from `openMarkets()` only, and keep the URL
  `?category=` deep link working by passing the category through.

### 10. Smaller items
- **"Rec" is a column of zeros in the default view.** Standard scoring sets
  `reception_multiplier` to 0, so `reception_points` is `0.0` for all 133
  rows. Hide the column when `scoring === "std"`.
  (`fantasy/index.html:69`, `fantasy/app.js:727`)
- **The Market Freshness card is the least informative freshness display
  on the page.** It renders "Kalshi — as of Aug 12" while Top Offenses,
  four inches above, renders "Kalshi (17 days ago)". The card named
  "freshness" should use `quoteAge`, which is what makes the staleness
  legible. (`fantasy/app.js:771`)
- **The board note hides its own truncation.** "10 of 100 · as of Aug 30"
  where 133 players are quoted; "100" reads as the total. Send the server's
  full count and say "10 of 133".
- **`popstate` doesn't restore focus.** Closing a drawer via the back
  button sets `hidden` directly without calling `restoreOverlayFocus()`,
  so focus is left on a hidden element and `overlayFocus` leaks.
  (`fantasy/app.js:1744`)
- **Nested interactive content in the market board.** Rows are
  `role="button"` with `tabindex=0` and contain a real `<button>`
  (`.row-compare`). Valid ARIA doesn't allow a button inside a button; the
  keyboard path also can't reach the inner one. (`fantasy/app.js:696`, `:557`)
- **Search results have no arrow-key navigation.** `#playerSearch` is a
  `role="combobox"` with `aria-controls`/`aria-autocomplete="list"`, but
  Up/Down do nothing — only Escape and Tab-then-Enter work.
  (`fantasy/app.js:232`)
- **`loadTeamOverview` can leave its button permanently disabled.** The
  `finally` block only re-enables when `!stale(generation)`, so a
  generation bump mid-flight strands `#teamOverviewRefresh` disabled until
  the next render. (`fantasy/league/app.js:675`)

## Resolutions — 2026-08-30

### Finding 1 — player search
`search_players` now ranks by (a) whether the term starts a word in the name
and (b) season-long projected points, then name. The two tiers are
deliberately coarse: grading "starts the whole name" above "starts a later
word" reads as correct and is not — it puts every first-name Allen above
Josh Allen — so the word-boundary test exists only to keep a buried match
("Hill" inside "Phillips") out of the running, and the projection decides
everything else. Season-long rather than the current week's run, because a
weekly run nulls out anyone who is out and dropped Tyreek Hill below every
Hilliard in the catalog. LIKE wildcards in the user's term are now escaped.

Live results: `Allen` → Josh Allen, Keenan Allen, LeQuint Allen; `Hill` →
Tyreek Hill, Justice Hill, Taysom Hill; `Smith` → Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Geno
Smith, DeVonta Smith; `Tucker` → Tucker Kraft; `Chase` → Ja'Marr Chase.

Three comments describing the shared search as alphabetical — in
`fantasy_rankings_board.search_rankable_players`, the `/rankings/players/search`
route, and `test_board_search_puts_the_best_player_first` — were rewritten,
since the difference between the two endpoints is now scope and scoring
rather than ordering.

### Finding 2 — the Edge column
`_score_season_player` now emits `missing_pairs` (a category the position is
expected to score in with no quote at all) beside the existing
`partial_pairs` (one half quoted), plus `edge_is_qualified`. The expected-pair
map is asymmetric on purpose — QB→rushing and RB→receiving are routinely tens
of points, while receivers and tight ends almost never rush, so flagging their
absent rushing market would put a warning on nearly every WR/TE row to no
purpose. On live data this flags the 23 rushing-only RBs and 10 passing-only
QBs that previously said nothing, and leaves all 41 receiving-only WRs and 15
TEs unflagged.

`seasonPairDetail` keeps the two states apart in copy — "no receiving market"
versus "receiving not fully quoted" — because "said nothing" and "said
something we could not use" are different claims. The Edge cell itself is
marked with a dotted underline and a tooltip naming what the total is built
from; it is qualified rather than withheld, since it remains the closest
comparison available. The compare drawer carries the same flag, and its
projection is now labelled "season proj" so it no longer collides with the
"wk 1 proj" three lines below it (a partial fix for finding 4).

The Methodology disclosure now states that Edge subtracts a projection
counting categories the market may not quote, plus the interceptions and
fumbles nothing here quotes at all.

### Finding 3 — `/games/{id}/lines/history`
The shadowed helper is renamed `_consensus_price_for_outcome` and now
delegates to the probability-space `_consensus_price` rather than taking an
arithmetic mean of American odds. A parametrized contract test walks all
three markets, and a second covers the unknown-game case. Verified failing
before the fix and passing after.
