What the file needs

Holdings need a Symbol and Quantity column. Add Cost Basis (total) or Average Cost to see your gain. A new holdings upload replaces the last one.

Transactions also need a Date, an Action (buy or sell) and a Price (or Amount). Dividends and transfers are skipped.

With both, trades dated after the holdings' "as of" day are added on top; earlier ones are already in the holdings, so they're kept but not counted twice.

Prices here are generated sample values, not live quotes.
