What the file needs

Holdings need a Symbol and Quantity column. Add Cost Basis (total) or Average Cost to see your gain. A new holdings upload replaces the last one.

Transactions also need a Date, an Action or Type (buy or sell) and a Price (or Amount). Dividends and transfers are skipped. Account, Source, Fees, Notes and a Realized gain/loss column are kept and shown too. Crypto like BTC is priced as crypto; a position with no ticker (an option, a name) is kept and valued at your last trade.

Uploading the same file again only adds trades that aren't already saved.

With both, trades dated after the holdings' "as of" day are added on top; earlier ones are already in the holdings, so they're kept but not counted twice.
